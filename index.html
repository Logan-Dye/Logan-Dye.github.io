Curriculum Vitae

I build multiplayer games in Unreal Engine, with C++ for gameplay and C# for the services behind it. My work has taken me from multiplayer VR platforms to two shipped VR survival titles and into the networking, AI, and physics that drive OP4.

From 2017 to 2021, I studied Unreal Engine, C++, systems architecture, and multiplayer under a private game development mentor. I used personal projects to learn how the engine handles gameplay and how game state moves across a network.

From February 2021 to February 2023, I worked as a Technical Programmer at Orbital XR, a startup building a multiplayer VR social platform. I led the technical design and built its core VR systems and Steam networking. I also built vehicle systems, AI for traffic and pedestrians, and the in-world systems for placing and purchasing ad space.

I joined Combat Waffle Studios in March 2023 and worked there through April 2026 as Lead Developer on Silent North VR and Ghosts of Tabor. Both titles shipped. On Silent North VR, I built a SQL-backed world loot system for a 37 km map that automatically generated loot across more than 1,000 locations and responded to player events. I also built an area-based AI pool that preloaded 1,000 zombies and placed them around cities and near players with minimal observed impact on server and client performance.

I built custom client-authoritative movement and a base building and ownership system that linked players and their storage to claimable locations. I also developed the survival system on a custom fork of Unreal Engine's Gameplay Ability System, including elevation-based freezing.

On Ghosts of Tabor, I led the bunker overhaul and built core parts of it. I worked with the backend lead on client-side storage, clothing selection, DLC retrieval, and kiosk delivery for loose gear. The overhaul added bunker power, GPU mining for in-game currency, medical supply crafting, and farming with planting and harvesting. I also contributed engine tooling and build pipeline infrastructure at Combat Waffle.

In April 2026, I joined Designful as Technical Project Lead and Technical Networking Lead for OP4. I designed the network architecture between Unreal Engine game servers and the C# backend and built the backend, matchmaker, Steam and custom email authentication, and persistent storage outside Unreal. I also implemented linear and custom XP progression, real-money purchasing, and Tarkov-style modular class and weapon building.

I develop the real-time ballistics and armor simulation for OP4, and I built its client-predicted networking and networked physics for ballistics. I also built the loot system and military squad AI that mimics players by flanking, stalking, looting bodies, and trading recovered gear.

Across those projects, I've used Unreal Engine 4 and 5, pairing C++ with Blueprints. My NPC work spans behavior trees, perception, navigation, and pathfinding. My backend and tooling work includes SQL, REST APIs, Linux servers, and Jenkins CI/CD.
